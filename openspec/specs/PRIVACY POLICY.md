PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: October 4, 2026
1. Information We Collect and Authentication
Because this is a closed, invite-only tool for known users, we do not collect public registrations. We only hold and process:
• Pre-Registered Information: The email address we already have on file for you.
• Authentication Logs: Temporary records of the emailed one-time codes used to verify your identity and log you into the system.
• Reservation Requests: Your preferred hut locations, dates, party size, and lottery entry preferences.
2. How We Use and Share Your Information
We use your information strictly to operate the reservation request lottery.
• Data Sharing for Bookings: Once winning requests are finalized, your name, contact information, and reservation details will be shared with Clair Tappaan Lodge (CTL) and any specific vendors used by CTL to transition your request into a live booking.
• No Commercial Sharing: We do not sell, lease, or rent your personal information to any other third parties or advertisers.
3. California-Specific Privacy Disclosures (CalOPPA / CCPA)
As a small, strictly non-commercial, volunteer-run service, this App does not sell personal data or meet the commercial thresholds of the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). However, we respect your privacy rights. Since we utilize your pre-existing email address to issue secure login tokens, you may contact the system administrator if you wish to see your logged request history or request data deletion.
4. Data Security
We protect your one-time code authentication path using standard secure protocols. However, because this tool is maintained entirely by volunteers, we cannot guarantee absolute immunity from data breaches or server failures.
